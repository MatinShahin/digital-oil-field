    Field oilWater cutWith AI optimisation

    Six wells drain one sandstone reservoir 2,300 m down: three beam pumps, two electric submersible pumps (one horizontal), and a water injector whose flood front sweeps oil toward the producers. Each producer declines at 12% a year; water breaks through when the front reaches it, so water cut climbs while oil falls. Fluids run through the manifold to a three-phase separator: oil to the tank farm and export line, gas to sales with a small flare, produced water back to injection. The AI optimiser rebalances chokes and injection and slows the ESP whose vibration is climbing. Model and data are illustrative.
